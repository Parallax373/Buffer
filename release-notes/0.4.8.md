Exporting a long clip to a small size is faster and far more reliable, and the editor says what it is doing.

## New
- **Editor:** a loading bar shows while a large clip opens instead of a black screen.
- **Editor:** when the target size is very small, the picture is scaled down automatically and the export panel tells you.
- **Clips:** click an export in the Exports popover or history to open it in your video app; drag it to take it into Discord.

## Improved
- **Editor:** the quality bar has a heading and more generous ratings, since Discord clips do not need to look like 4K.
- **Editor:** exports that miss the target now say so, and show when a clip needed extra passes.
- **Clips:** small targets no longer fail, they are encoded at the lowest quality that fits and flagged instead.
- **Clips:** exports are faster, with fewer repeat passes on long clips.

## Fixed
- **Recording:** a recording whose picture freezes (screen capture lost) restarts itself instead of saving clips of old audio over a still frame.
