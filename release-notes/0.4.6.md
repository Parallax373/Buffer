The Fans page now shows your case like the RGB page does, with live fans and an airflow estimate, and the RGB page got a round of fixes.

## New
- **Fans:** the Case view and the Detect dialog use the same case as the RGB page (same templates and places, 3D and 2D, fullscreen from the corner of the view). The fans turn with their live speed, a click picks a fan, and the Placement tab lists the real places of your case.
- **Fans:** an Airflow switch shows air going from the intake fans to the exhaust fans, turning orange near a hot CPU or graphics card, and a chip says whether the case has positive, negative or balanced pressure. Each fan can be set to blow in or out. It is an estimate from fan size and speed, not a simulation of air.
- **RGB:** colour calibration has two dials, Warmth (cooler to warmer) and Tint (greener to pinker), so a card that looks too yellow is fixed by moving one dial. The red, green and blue lines are still there under Channels.
- **RGB:** the 3D / 2D view in Advanced can go fullscreen from the button in its top right corner (Esc leaves).
- **Settings:** the audio offset has a slider next to the number field.

## Improved
- **RGB:** "Under the desk" is now an On the desk | Under the desk switch that shows where the case stands.
- **RGB:** in Advanced, Like the rest also gives the device the colour most other devices show, not only full brightness and no delay.
- **RGB:** long device names in the Advanced list wrap onto a second line instead of being cut off, and the list is a little wider.
- **RGB:** the calibration window no longer has a Reference button (it did nothing) or a grey test colour; the test brightness slider shows how a colour holds up when dim.

## Fixed
- **RGB:** loading a profile no longer switches you to the Simple view, and switching view no longer marks the profile as changed.
- **RGB:** after the PC sleeps, a motherboard whose lights stopped answering comes back: Buffer restarts OpenRGB after waking, and Scan again restarts it too.
- **RGB:** devices that OpenRGB wrongly reports as already in Direct mode (a mouse left in its own effect) now take Buffer's colours. Thanks to @Parallax373.
- **Fans:** MSI Afterburner no longer pauses the graphics card just because it is running; it only does so while its own software fan curve is on.
- **Fans:** on a laptop that does not allow apps to change the fan speed, the page says so instead of showing an empty list. Thanks to @Parallax373.
