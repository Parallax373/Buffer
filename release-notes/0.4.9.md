Fan setup is easier to follow, and the case view now makes heat and airflow clearer.

## New
- **Fans:** the case view can use your CPU and graphics-card power readings to show where the air is warming up.
- **Fans:** while assigning fans, choose whether each selected position is intake or exhaust.

## Improved
- **Fans:** exhausts and fan speed are easier to distinguish in the case view, with a clearer legend and an on-screen paused state.
- **Fans:** the Assign fans guide can briefly stop a fan instead of making it run at full speed, and points to the position being edited.

## Fixed
- **Fans:** an administrator step that gets stuck now stops after four minutes instead of leaving the app waiting indefinitely.
- **Installer:** starting development mode no longer fails when the recorder from a previous run still has its executable open.
