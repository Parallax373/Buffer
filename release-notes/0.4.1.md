FanControl no longer keeps Buffer's fan control paused after you close it.

## New
- **Fans:** a Close FanControl button next to "Paused: FanControl is running". FanControl keeps a background service driving the fans even with its tray icon gone; the button closes FanControl, stops that service and stops it from starting with Windows, so Buffer takes over within seconds.

## Heads-up
- **Fans:** the button asks for administrator approval once. FanControl stays installed: open it again whenever you want to use it.
