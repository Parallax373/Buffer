A forgotten manual recording can no longer run for days, and closing the recorder on purpose now sticks.

## New
- **Recording:** a manual recording (Alt+R) shows a reminder every 2 hours and stops and saves by itself after 12 hours.

## Fixed
- **Recording:** a recording that was cut short (power loss, sleep) is recovered as one clip again, not split into many.
- **Recorder:** closing the recorder from the tray no longer brings it back a few seconds later. It still comes back after a crash.
- **Settings:** in the Library tab the remove and change buttons for folders are back on the folder's row.
