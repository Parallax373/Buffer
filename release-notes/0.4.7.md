Buffer's first-run setup is clearer and quicker, with friendlier guidance for recording, RGB and fan control.

## New
- **Fans:** the Simple view groups your processor, graphics card and case fans, with one Quiet-to-Cool dial for each group; the Advanced view adds a clearer editor and live status.
- **Fans:** a new case airflow view shows fan direction, PC temperatures and an estimate of whether intake and exhaust are balanced.
- **Editor:** the export panel explains how to improve a low-quality export and keeps the codec choices visible.

## Improved
- **Settings:** first run starts in your Windows language, and the four-step setup can be closed and resumed whenever you are ready.
- **RGB:** when no devices are found, Buffer now offers a clear way to leave the setup and come back later.
- **Recording:** saving a shortcut shows a countdown and explains when another app is already using that key.

## Fixed
- **Recording:** fresh installs no longer show a false source-unavailable warning, and recording-install errors are explained in your language.
- **RGB:** installation errors now use short, understandable codes and are shown in the app's language.
- **Fans:** removing the fan helper or OpenRGB asks for confirmation first.
